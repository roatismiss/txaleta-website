This Cookie Policy explains which cookies and similar technologies are used on www.txaletadecamiguin.com, what they do, and how you can control them.

> **At a glance**
>
> - We use **no analytics, advertising or cross-site tracking cookies.**
> - The website itself works almost entirely without cookies. What it does store is essential: your cookie choices, and what our secure booking engine needs.
> - The only optional item is **Instagram content**, which loads only if you allow it.
> - You can change your choice at any time with **Cookie settings**, at the bottom of every page.

## 1. What cookies are

Cookies are small text files that a website saves in your browser. Similar technologies, such as **local storage** and **session storage**, keep small pieces of information in your browser in the same way. In this policy, "cookies" covers all of them.

Some cookies are **essential**: a website or a service you ask for cannot work without them. Others are **optional**, and under the laws that apply to us they may be used only with your consent.

## 2. How we use them

We sort cookies into two categories.

**Essential — always on.** These remember your cookie choices, run our secure booking and payment engine, and keep our concierge chat working. They do not track you across other websites, and the law does not require consent for them. You can block them in your browser, but parts of the website, booking in particular, may then stop working.

**External media — off until you allow it.** Some pages show posts from **Instagram**. Loading a post sends your IP address and the address of the page to Meta Platforms, which runs Instagram, and allows Meta to set or read its own cookies. We load this content only after you have said yes, either in the cookie banner or by choosing to load a single post.

We do not use analytics, advertising, retargeting or social-tracking cookies. If we ever want to, we will update this policy and ask for your consent first.

## 3. The cookies on this website

| Name | Set by | Purpose | Category | Duration |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `txaleta_consent` (cookie) | Txaleta | Remembers your cookie choices, so we do not ask on every page | Essential | 12 months |
| `txaleta:splash-seen` (session storage) | Txaleta | Remembers that the opening animation has played, so it runs only once per visit | Essential | Until you close the browser tab |
| Booking engine storage (cookies and local storage) | Cloudbeds, on the booking page only | Runs the booking session: keeps your dates, room and checkout working, secures payment and helps prevent fraud | Essential | Session, or as set by Cloudbeds |
| `cr-widget-sound` (local storage, inside the chat window) | CloudReef, our concierge chat | Remembers whether you turned the chat's sound off. Set only if you change it | Essential | Until you clear your browser storage |
| Instagram cookies | Meta Platforms (instagram.com) | Displays embedded Instagram posts. Meta may use its cookies under its own policy | External media (optional) | As set by Meta |

Third parties set their own cookies and decide how long they last. For details, see the [Cloudbeds privacy policy](https://www.cloudbeds.com/privacy-policy/) and the [Instagram privacy policy](https://privacycenter.instagram.com/policy).

## 4. Services that load on this website

To show you this website, your browser connects to the following services. They receive technical information such as your IP address, as any web server does, but **we do not use them to set tracking cookies**.

- **Vercel** hosts the website.
- **Cloudflare** delivers our videos.
- **CloudReef** provides the concierge chat. It loads quietly in the background, so that it opens instantly when you need it.
- **Cloudbeds** provides the booking engine on the booking page, together with the services Cloudbeds itself relies on to run it.
- **Instagram**, on pages that show Instagram posts, and only after you allow it.

Our fonts are served from our own domain, so your browser does not contact Google Fonts.

## 5. How to change your choices

- Use **Cookie settings**, at the bottom of every page, to allow or refuse external media at any time. Your new choice applies straight away.
- You can also block or delete cookies in your browser settings. Blocking essential cookies may stop the booking engine from working.

When we add a new kind of cookie that needs consent, we will ask you again.

## 6. Changes to this policy

We will update this policy whenever the cookies on our website change. The date at the top of this page shows when it last changed.
