## 7. Your safety on the clifftop

Txaleta sits on a clifftop above the Bohol Sea, with an infinity pool, open terraces and a path down the cliff to a rocky shore. Natural settings bring uneven ground, steps, wet rock, changing sea conditions and wildlife.

- Use the pool, the cliff path and the shore with care, and follow posted signs and the instructions of our staff.
- Children must be supervised by an adult at all times near the pool, the cliff edge and the sea.
- Do not swim after drinking alcohol, at night, or when we advise against it. Ask us about sea conditions before entering the water.
- Camiguin is a volcanic island, and Mt. Hibok-Hibok is an active volcano. We follow the advisories of PHIVOLCS and the local authorities, and we ask you to follow our instructions in any emergency.
- Please tell us about any medical condition that may affect your stay, so that we can help you if needed.
