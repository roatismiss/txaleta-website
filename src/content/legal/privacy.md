This Privacy Policy explains what personal information Txaleta de Camiguin collects when you book, stay with us, contact us or visit this website, why we collect it, who we share it with, and the rights you have over it.

> **At a glance**
>
> - We collect only what we need to welcome you, and we **never sell or rent** your information.
> - Reservations and online payments are processed by **Cloudbeds**. Card details are handled within its secure, PCI DSS-compliant environment. **We never see or store your full card number.**
> - This website uses **no analytics, advertising or cross-site tracking cookies.**
> - Our concierge chat is AI-assisted. Your messages go to our guest-communication platform and its AI providers, and only to answer you.
> - For everyone's safety, **CCTV operates in parts of the resort**, never in guest rooms or bathrooms.
> - You can ask us at any time to see, correct or delete your information.

## 1. Who is responsible for your information

Txaleta de Camiguin, Purok 6, Puting Balas, Mambajao, 9100 Camiguin, Philippines, is responsible for your personal information. Under Philippine law we are the "personal information controller". Under the EU and UK General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), where it applies, we are the "controller".

We handle personal information in line with the **Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173)**, its Implementing Rules and Regulations and the issuances of the National Privacy Commission. Where the GDPR applies to you, for example because you live in the European Union or the United Kingdom, we also follow it.

For any question or request about your personal information, write to us at [txaletadecamiguin@gmail.com](mailto:txaletadecamiguin@gmail.com), marking your message "Privacy". It will be handled by the member of our team responsible for data protection.

## 2. The information we collect

### When you book directly with us

Your name, email address, phone number and country; your arrival and departure dates, room, rate and number of guests; and any requests or messages you add, such as your expected arrival time.

Payment details are entered directly into Cloudbeds' secure payment form. We receive the information we need to manage your reservation, such as the payment status and, where Cloudbeds makes it available, the card type and last four digits. We never receive your full card number.

### When you stay with us

The details required to register guests, including information from your identity document; the names of the people staying with you; your charges for meals, rentals, activities and other services; and preferences you choose to share with us.

If you tell us about **dietary needs, allergies or a medical condition**, we treat this as sensitive personal information and use it only to look after you safely during your stay, with your consent.

For vehicle and watercraft rentals, we may record your driving licence details.

### CCTV

For the safety of our guests and team, and to protect the property, **CCTV cameras operate in parts of the resort**, such as entrances and common areas. They record images of people in those areas. Cameras are never placed inside guest rooms or bathrooms.

### When you use our concierge chat

The messages you write, the language you write in, the page you were on, and any contact details you choose to give us, such as your name, email address or phone number, so that our team can follow up.

### When you contact us

Messages and contact details you send by email, phone, WhatsApp, Viber, Facebook or Instagram.

### When you browse this website

Our hosting and content-delivery providers automatically record technical information, such as your IP address, browser and device type, the pages requested and the time of the request. This information is used to keep the website secure and running. **We do not use it to build profiles, and we do not run analytics or advertising trackers.** For cookies and similar technologies, see our [Cookie Policy](/cookies).

### From others

If you book through an online travel agency such as Booking.com or Agoda, or through a travel agent, they send us the details needed to manage your reservation. If someone else books for you, they give us your name and the details of your stay.

## 3. Why we use it, and our legal basis

| Purpose | Legal basis |

| Answering your questions by chat, email, phone or messaging | Your request, and our legitimate interest in replying to you (Sec. 12(b) and 12(f); Art. 6(1)(b) and 6(1)(f)) |

| Sending practical information before your arrival, and a thank-you or feedback request after your stay | Our legitimate interest in looking after our guests. You can opt out at any time (Sec. 12(f); Art. 6(1)(f)) |
| Sending news and offers | Only with your consent, which you can withdraw at any time (Sec. 12(a); Art. 6(1)(a)) |
| Keeping our website, systems and payments secure, and preventing fraud | Our legitimate interest in protecting our guests and our business (Sec. 12(f); Art. 6(1)(f)) |
| CCTV in parts of the resort, for the safety of guests and team and the security of the property | Our legitimate interest in keeping people and property safe (Sec. 12(f); Art. 6(1)(f)) |
| Showing Instagram posts on our pages | Your consent, given in the cookie settings (Sec. 12(a); Art. 6(1)(a)) |

## 4. Who we share it with

We share personal information only where needed for the purposes above. **We never sell or rent it.**

**Service providers who act on our instructions:**

- **Cloudbeds** — our booking engine, reservation system and channel manager, and the processor of online payments. See the [Cloudbeds privacy policy](https://www.cloudbeds.com/privacy-policy/).
- **Payment partners** — the banks, card networks, e-wallets and payment processors that Cloudbeds works with to complete your payment and prevent fraud.
- **CloudReef** — the guest-communication platform that runs our concierge chat and stores enquiries for our team.
- **AI model providers** — currently **OpenAI** and **Anthropic**, which process chat messages to generate the concierge's replies. They act under commercial API terms that do not allow them to use your messages to train their models by default.
- **Vercel** (website hosting) and **Cloudflare** (delivery of video and media).
- **Google** — our email service.

**Partners who deliver services you book**, such as boat, dive, tour and transfer operators and local guides. They receive only what they need, for example the name for a booking, the number of people and a pick-up time.

**Booking platforms** such as Booking.com and Agoda, when you booked through them, to manage that reservation.

**Messaging platforms** such as WhatsApp, Viber, Facebook and Instagram, when you choose to contact us through them. Their own privacy policies apply to their services.

**Authorities and advisers.** Government authorities when the law requires it, including CCTV footage requested under a lawful order or needed to report an incident, and professional advisers, such as accountants and lawyers, who are bound by confidentiality.

## 5. International transfers

Some of our service providers store or process information outside the Philippines, for example in the United States or the European Union. We work with providers that protect personal information through contractual commitments and, where the GDPR applies, through recognised safeguards such as the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses. Under the Data Privacy Act we remain accountable for your information when it is processed by others on our behalf.

## 6. How long we keep it

- **Reservation, stay and billing records** are kept for as long as Philippine tax, accounting and guest-registration rules require, and then deleted or anonymised.
- **Enquiries and chat conversations** that do not lead to a stay are kept only for as long as we need them to answer you and follow up on your enquiry, and then deleted.
- **CCTV recordings** are kept for a limited period and then automatically overwritten, unless a recording is needed to investigate an incident or the law requires us to keep it.
- **Server logs** are kept by our hosting providers for short periods, for security and troubleshooting.
- **Marketing preferences** are kept until you withdraw your consent, and then we keep a record that you opted out, so we do not contact you again.
- **Sensitive information** such as allergies or health details is deleted once it is no longer needed for your stay.

## 7. How we protect it

All information sent to and from this website is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). Card details never pass through our own systems. Access to guest information and CCTV recordings is limited to the members of our team who need it, and they are bound by confidentiality. We choose service providers that maintain strong security standards.

If a personal data breach occurs that is likely to put you at risk, we will notify the National Privacy Commission and the people affected, as the law requires, and within 72 hours of becoming aware of it where that applies.

## 8. Your rights

Under the Data Privacy Act, you have the right to:

- **be informed** about how your personal information is used;
- **access** the information we hold about you;
- **correct** information that is inaccurate or incomplete;
- **object** to processing, including for direct marketing;
- have your information **erased or blocked**, where the law allows;
- **data portability**, to receive your information in a commonly used electronic format;
- **claim damages** for harm caused by inaccurate, incomplete, outdated, false or unlawfully obtained or used information; and
- **file a complaint** with the National Privacy Commission.

If the GDPR applies to you, you also have the right to restrict processing, to withdraw your consent at any time without affecting what was done before, and to complain to the data protection authority in the country where you live or work.

To exercise any of these rights, including a request to see CCTV footage of yourself, email [txaletadecamiguin@gmail.com](mailto:txaletadecamiguin@gmail.com). We may ask you to confirm your identity before acting on a request, and we protect the privacy of other people who appear in any recording we share. We reply within one month, and requests are free of charge in most cases.

If you are not satisfied with our answer, you can contact the **National Privacy Commission** of the Philippines at [privacy.gov.ph](https://privacy.gov.ph/).

## 9. Children

Reservations are made by adults. We collect information about children only when a parent or guardian gives it to us for their stay, such as names and ages for the room's occupancy.
